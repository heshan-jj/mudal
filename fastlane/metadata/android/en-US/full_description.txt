Your bank already texts you every transaction. Mudal reads those SMS on your phone and turns them into an automatic expense tracker — no manual entry, no sign-up, nothing uploaded.

★ Why Mudal asks for SMS permission
Reading bank SMS is how the app works — it is the entire product. Everything happens on your device: no server ever receives a message, there is no account to create, and the complete source code is public (AGPL-3.0) so anyone can verify exactly what it does. Would rather not grant it? You can still add transactions by hand or import a PDF statement.

★ Automatic expense tracking
• Every card payment, ATM withdrawal and bank transfer becomes a categorized transaction
• 5 banks and payment services across 1 country, with multi-currency support
• Works with any bank app — it reads your bank's SMS, not the app
• A clean, searchable spending timeline that stays up to date on its own

★ Built for Sri Lanka
• Parses Sampath Bank, Commercial Bank of Ceylon, National Development Bank, National Savings Bank and Nations Trust Bank
• Rupee amounts, dates and number formats the way Sri Lankan banks send them
• Defaults to LKR, with your own categories and rules on top

★ Budgets and insights
• Set monthly budgets per category and watch progress as you spend
• Spending trends, category breakdowns and month-on-month comparisons
• Recurring transactions and subscriptions, so you can see what is coming before it lands
• Smart rules to auto-categorize transactions your way

★ Optional AI assistant
Ask "what did I spend on food last month?" and get a real answer. Mudal talks to Google Gemini using your own free API key, which you enter in Settings. Without a key the assistant stays switched off and the app makes no AI requests.

★ Private by design
• No account, no login, no email required
• No ads, no trackers, no analytics on your financial data
• Your database is encrypted on the device
• SMS parsing, categorisation and budgets never leave the device
• Open source (AGPL-3.0) — read the code yourself

★ Completely free
Every feature is unlocked: unlimited rules, PDF statement imports, CSV export, duplicate-account merge and scheduled daily backups. No paywall, no in-app purchases, no Pro tier.

A money manager, budget planner and spending tracker in one — for people who hate typing in expenses.
